\section{Scope and Practical Guidance}
\label{sec:limitations}

Our conclusions apply to nine-feature heads trained on 30 to 90 per-cell
(at most 959 pooled) trajectories in four-search prefix replay over static
English corpora with mostly sparse retrieval. Longer horizons, fresh
same-population trajectories, retrieval stacks,
languages, hidden-state features, and joint control require new matched
frontiers. We refit each head per cell, so crossed-backbone and news results
test recurrence rather than frozen transfer. FBG labels one realized
continuation rather than a counterfactual value.

Unweighted counts summarize heterogeneous cells. The nested bootstrap
conditions on logged trajectories and decoding, while independent draws test
population variation. In low-EM cells, we evaluate controller differences
rather than answer quality. Our execution ledger excludes lifecycle, invoice,
energy, retry, and failure costs. The supplement reports tail latency. Answer
repetition is competitive only when prefix answers exist, and other cost
structures require a separate frontier.


To transfer this audit, declare observable state and executed costs, select
references on development data, freeze policies, and collect independent test
questions. Match each cost ledger and report paired and simultaneous intervals.
Keep replay separate from online adaptive-query evidence. Fresh
same-population training beyond 90 trajectories and longer horizons remain
untested.
